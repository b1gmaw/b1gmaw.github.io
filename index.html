02 // dossier

About me

I came to Japan to study the language, passed JLPT N1, and went on to earn a Business Administration degree at Ryukoku University. I now work in the international department of a Japanese language school. Programming started on the side and turned into the thing I spend most evenings on.

Living between four languages makes the rough edges of Japanese web software hard to miss: forms that reject your input over invisible character widths, admin work held together by spreadsheets. I like building the tool that removes the friction, then making it solid enough that other people rely on it every day.

active TypeScript · pushed 2026-10-02

dejapanify

A browser extension that fixes the unintuitive parts of Japanese websites, starting with the 半角・全角 guessing game on forms. It reads what each field actually requires — the pattern attribute, the printed hint, the placeholder example — and converts your input to match.

  • Chromium + Firefox
  • no runtime dependencies
  • no network access
